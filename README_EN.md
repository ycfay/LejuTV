# LeJuTV (乐聚)

**One app. Multiple platforms. Simple viewing.**

LeJuTV is a lightweight video aggregation app that brings short videos, live streams, and TV channels together in one simple interface. It integrates content from Douyin, Kuaishou, Douyu, Bilibili, and online TV broadcasts, making it easier to find and watch your favorite content without constantly switching between apps.

Designed with simplicity in mind, LeJuTV aims to provide an accessible and immersive viewing experience on Android TV, Android phones, and tablets.

## ✨ Features

- **Multi-Platform Content** — Access video and live-streaming platforms in one place.
- **TV Live Channels** — Organize online TV streams into an easy-to-browse channel list.
- **Simple Interface** — Find your favorite content with minimal effort.
- **Immersive Viewing** — Enjoy a consistent viewing experience across different screen sizes.
- **Multiple Devices** — Supports Android TV, Android phones, and tablets.
- **AI-Driven Development** — Built with extensive assistance from OpenAI Codex.
